# Publication checklist

## Evidence
- [ ] Every material claim maps to an inspected source.
- [ ] Dates, names, quotations, versions and numbers are verified.
- [ ] Conflicting evidence and uncertainty remain visible.
- [ ] Every cited URL opens and supports the adjacent claim.

## Editorial
- [ ] The hook connects to the actual argument.
- [ ] Every section advances the thesis.
- [ ] A dedicated evidence-safe humanization pass was reconciled.
- [ ] The final draft passed an oral/rhythm edit.

## Visuals
- [ ] Original illustration created and inspected.
- [ ] Informative visual created from documented provenance.
- [ ] Explanatory diagram created and inspected.
- [ ] Captions, alt text and mobile exports verified.

## Publication
- [ ] Platform rules were checked recently.
- [ ] Exact destination, account, title and settings were reviewed.
- [ ] Explicit human publication approval received.
- [ ] Public URL and rendered article independently verified after publication.
