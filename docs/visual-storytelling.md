# Visual storytelling contract

## Required roles

For substantial articles, plan three distinct functions:

1. **Editorial illustration:** captures the central tension or concept.
2. **Informative visual:** communicates evidence, chronology or comparison.
3. **Explanatory diagram:** explains a process, architecture, sequence or relationship.

One asset may satisfy two roles only when it genuinely performs both. Decorative stock imagery does not count as an informative visual.

## Asset record

For every visual, record:

- reader question;
- claim or information communicated;
- source data and provenance;
- visual type and rationale;
- placement and caption;
- dimensions and export formats;
- alt text and text equivalent;
- creator, license and reuse conditions.

## Integrity and accessibility

Use readable labels, semantic colors and one obvious reading direction. Include units, scales, legends and dates where relevant. Avoid misleading axes and cherry-picked ranges.

Inspect exported pixels at desktop and mobile widths. Source SVG or HTML correctness does not prove the rendered image has no clipping, overlap, low contrast or ambiguous connectors.

Do not present generated scenes as documentary evidence. Follow the destination's current disclosure requirements.
