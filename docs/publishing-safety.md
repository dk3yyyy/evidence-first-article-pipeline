# Publishing safety

## Separate approvals

Treat these as distinct actions:

- approving article text;
- staging a private draft;
- submitting for editorial review;
- publishing publicly;
- emailing subscribers;
- sending app notifications;
- cross-posting to another destination.

Authorization for one does not imply authorization for the others.

## Pre-publication gate

Before the final action, show the human owner:

- destination and account;
- exact title and subtitle;
- final article and assets;
- tags, canonical settings and visibility;
- email and notification settings;
- required disclosures;
- unresolved checks.

## Post-publication verification

Verify the clean reader URL without relying only on an authenticated editor. Confirm title, author, body, links, images, alt text, canonical settings and the absence of accidental duplicate sections. Do not report success from a confirmation dialog alone.
